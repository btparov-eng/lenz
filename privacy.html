Privacy policy

Applies to the Lenz Android app and the Lenz Windows app. Last updated: 11 September 2026.

The short version

Lenz does not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no Lenz servers. Your video travels only between your own phone and your own PC, on your local network, encrypted.

Camera video

The phone app uses the camera only while a paired PC asks it to stream. The video is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and sent directly to that PC over your local network. It is not recorded, uploaded or sent to anyone else. On the PC, the picture is shown in the preview and offered to the apps you choose through the Windows camera system; Windows camera privacy settings apply to those apps.

Data kept on your devices

WhatWhereWhy
Pairing keys and device names (for example “Pixel 8”, the PC's computer name)On the phone, and on the PC protected by Windows (DPAPI)So paired devices recognise each other. Removed with Forget or by uninstalling.
Your settings (resolution, looks, overlays, hotkeys, images you chose)PC: %AppData%\LenzTo remember your choices.
A technical log (errors, versions, connection events)PC: %AppData%\Lenz\app.logFor troubleshooting. It never leaves your PC unless you copy it yourself with Copy diagnostics.

Network connections

  • Local network only, for the camera. The phone announces itself on your WiFi (a small broadcast with its name and a random device identifier) so the PC can find it. The camera connection itself stays on your local network.
  • Update check (PC, optional). Once a day the PC app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists (api.github.com). The request contains only the Lenz version number. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. You can turn this off in About & diagnostics. Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically.
  • Nothing else. Lenz makes no other internet connections.

Android permissions

  • Camera — to film, only while streaming to a paired PC.
  • Network / WiFi state — to be found by the PC and to stream to it on your local network.
  • Notifications and foreground service — so streaming continues with the screen off, and to ask you to allow a new PC.
  • Wake lock — to keep streaming while the screen is off.

Children

Lenz collects no data from anyone, including children.
