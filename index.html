Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Lenz is free during the beta, with no watermark and no resolution limit. If a paid version ever comes, it will add new features; what you have now stays free.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen does not know it. Click More info → Run anyway. Every release lists the SHA-256 of the file so you can check it.

Android warns about installing an unknown app

The app is not on the Play Store yet. Allow your browser or file manager to install it once. A Play Store version is in testing.

Is my video sent anywhere?

No. It goes straight from your phone to your PC over your local network, encrypted. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Does it add delay?

The phone encodes with its hardware, the PC decodes on the graphics card with low-latency settings. On a normal 5 GHz WiFi it feels like a wired webcam.

The phone does not show up on the PC

Check both are on the same WiFi and that the network is set to Private in Windows. You can also type the address the phone shows. Guest networks often block devices from seeing each other.

Does it do audio?

Not yet. Lenz is video only; keep using your headset or PC microphone.

Something broke. How do I report it?

In the PC app open About & diagnostics → Copy diagnostics and paste the result into a GitHub issue.
