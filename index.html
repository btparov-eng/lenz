Set up in two minutes

Both devices on the same WiFi network, that's the only requirement.

Install both apps

Run the Windows installer, then install the APK on your phone (allow installs from this source when Android asks).

Pair once

Open Lenz on both. Your phone appears on the PC: press Connect, check the same code shows on both screens, and accept.

Pick “Lenz Camera”

In Discord, OBS, Teams, Zoom or your browser, choose Lenz Camera. Next time it connects by itself.

PC

  • Windows 11 (22H2 or newer), 64-bit
  • Any DirectX 11 graphics card (integrated is fine)
  • The installer adds the .NET 8 runtime if needed

Phone

  • Android 9 or newer
  • 5 GHz WiFi recommended above 1080p
  • Keep it charging for long sessions: Lenz watches battery and temperature

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Lenz is free during the beta, with no watermark and no resolution limit. If a paid version ever comes, it will add new features; what you have now stays free.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC” / unknown publisher

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen does not know it. Click More info → Run anyway. You can check the file's SHA-256 against the one on the release page.

Android warns about installing an unknown app

The app is not on the Play Store yet. Allow your browser or file manager to install it once. A Play Store version is on the way.

Is my video sent anywhere?

No. The video goes straight from your phone to your PC over your local network, encrypted. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

The phone does not show up on the PC

Check both are on the same WiFi, and that the network is set to Private in Windows. You can also type the address the phone shows. Guest networks often block devices from seeing each other.

Does it do audio?

Not yet. Lenz is video only; keep using your headset or PC microphone.

Something broke. How do I report it?

In the PC app open About & diagnostics → Copy diagnostics and paste the result into a GitHub issue.
